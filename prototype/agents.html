How meetings work

Start a meeting to get a link. The meeting agent joins your video room to present slides or talk through a published flow. With notes on, you get a transcript, summary and action items afterwards. Room time uses your free minutes first; agent time is charged from the wallet.

How personal agents work

Give an agent a goal instead of a script. It plans the steps, then calls, messages and looks things up from its own number until the job is done. Inside each task’s limits it works on its own; anything it can’t undo waits for you, and payments need your second factor.
