Silhouette and glyph tile carry the phase; colour carries only state. Capsules enter and leave the call, rectangles do the work. Spec §5.5 · F-FLOW-008, F-A11Y-008

Step text is set in flow units, so it would shrink with zoom. The glyph tile and phase word survive every band, so Logic and Action never collapse into identical boxes.

What each band keeps

  • Full: phase line with type and #, title, 2-line summary, meta, answer rows with examples, badges with words.
  • Compact: tile, phase word, one-line title, answer labels; badges become icon + count.
  • Block: silhouette and tile in the box; the title becomes a Block label (#n + title, 140 px, two lines) that may run into the gap; sockets stay as stubs; edge labels hide.
  • Zooming: during a pinch text simply scales; the 12 px clamp is re-applied once, when the gesture ends.
  • Band changes swap instantly with 0.03 hysteresis, never animated.

Socket states

  • Connected: filled socket colour with a 2 px surface ring; on a selected step, accent-mark.
  • Free: hollow, 1.5 px control border (≥ 3:1). Hover or focus adds the "+" Add step button 12 px beyond it.
  • Focused: the global 2 px focus outline, offset 2. Enter selects its connection; C opens Connect to…; Delete removes the connection.
  • Required, not connected: warning-soft fill, warning border, a 20 px stub and the words "Not connected". The step's error badge and Issues tab carry the fix.
  • Input port: left edge on the title line. Hollow while nothing reaches the step; arrowheads stop 2 px short of it.
  • The shaded circle marks the 24 px hit area for review only; it is never drawn in the product.
