5 · Version history and compare with live

§4.5 compare · §6 history, restore, roll back · F-FLOW-012, -017

Versions are numbers with an author, a date range, a note and how they were tested. Compare mode marks the canvas itself: Added steps take the success tint and the word Added, Changed steps a surface-3 header with a 2 px ink bar and the word Changed, so neither can be mistaken for the selected step; Removed steps are ghosts with a solid edge. Dashes stay reserved for fallback paths. History lives in the left panel so the inspector stays free.
