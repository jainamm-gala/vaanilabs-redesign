Data and navigation components

Sutradhar gallery for the data-nav group. Every board is rendered twice from one source: light on the left, dark on the right (wide boards stack). States that need a pointer or keyboard are forced with is-hover, is-focus and is-press classes so they can be reviewed side by side. Spec: spec/02-components-data-nav.md. Sample data only; no real customers.
