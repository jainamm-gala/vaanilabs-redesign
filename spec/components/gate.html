Gate

The one component in front of anything that dials, bills or goes live (direction P3). It has four parts: what will happen, the checks, the consequence line and one confirming action, confirmed with the labelled button or Ctrl Enter. Spec: spec/02-components-gate.md. Every component on this page comes from components.css; the page adds layout only (gg-). Sample data only.

1 · Check kinds (G §2.1)

One enum everywhere: pass · blocking · adjusted · advisory · checking · unknown. Red means fix this before confirming, amber means something changed or is risky, and neutral means worth knowing. Each row starts with a hidden kind word, so the state never depends on colour.

2 · CallGate: single (Cockpit) and batch (Leads)

A popover gate at 400 px: --e3, radius 12, no scrim, and focus starts on the title. In the Cockpit the scope is read-only (it was chosen on the card); from Leads it is editable. Checks sit under group labels. The consequence line sits above the choice, and the choice sits above the footer because it relabels the primary.

3 · Blocked, global blockers, changed

An in-gate blocker disables the primary: it stays focusable and reads its reason, which is the why-text in the danger tone. A global blocker such as wallet ₹0 keeps the gate closed, and its entry point says why (G §4.4). If the wallet drops to ₹0 while the gate is open, a blocking row appears at the top and the gate stays open. After 120 s the gate re-checks before confirming.

4 · Sheet gates: Publish and money

A 640 px sheet, modal with a flat scrim. On tablets it is full height and on phones full screen. The Publish gate reads decision, then blast radius, then detail. A warning needs a tick and relabels the button. The money gate's consequence line is the server quote, and its primary names the amount charged; nothing is charged before the UPI PIN.

5 · SetupTrack (page variant) and the inline checklist

Setup steps are checks whose fix is a whole task. An unmet step shows as a ring, not a red cross, because on a new workspace that is expected; the red x is kept for a step that actually failed. The inline checklist above a gate hides passing rows behind "Show all" (collapse="passing").

6 · Phone: the Call gate as a bottom sheet

Below 768 px, and on tablets when the gate doesn't fit its anchor, the popover gate becomes a bottom sheet over a flat scrim. Its header is sticky, and the primary is sticky above the safe area at 44 px. The content and rules are the same as on desktop.
