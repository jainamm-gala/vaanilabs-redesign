Overlays and feedback that tell the truth

Every container a Vaani operator meets when something opens, loads, fails, succeeds or needs a decision. Each specimen is rendered twice, in the light and dark themes, from the same tokens. The lightest container wins, focus always comes back, and nothing reports a state the system has not proven.

Dialog

§2 · F-A11Y-005, F-A11Y-027, F-VIS-022

Short modal tasks only: sm 400, md 560, lg 720. Flat scrim, radius 12, e3 and the overlay ring in dark. Focus goes to the first field and comes back to the trigger. A dirty dialog never throws away what was typed: the footer asks in place instead of stacking a second modal.

Confirmation and destructive actions

§3 · F-FLOW-001, F-FLOW-019, F-UX-035

The guard matches the risk: nothing, Undo, a confirm that names the object and consequence, a typed confirmation for irreversible or live objects, and the Call or Publish gate for anything that bills or goes live. Danger is an outline everywhere; there is no filled red in the product.

Sheet

§4 · F-UX-010, F-UX-032, F-A11Y-002, F-A11Y-010

Records open beside their list: 440 for a lead, 560 for a call, 640 for the Publish gate, 320 to 480 for the Flow inspector. Docked at 1440 and up, overlaying at 1024 to 1439, modal below. One scroll container, a sticky header with previous and next, everyday actions in the footer and Delete in the overflow menu.

Popover and tooltip

§5, §6 · F-VIS-014, F-VIS-015, F-VIS-013, F-A11Y-024

Popovers hold small anchored tasks and can contain links; tooltips only name, hint, reveal a truncated value or give a disabled control's reason. Tooltips are max-content up to 280 px, so one word per line cannot happen, and they render in a portal so no card or rail clips them.

Menu and context menu

§7 · F-A11Y-011, F-FLOW-019, F-UX-046

Items are 32 px (44 on touch) with a 16 px icon column, keycaps on the right and descriptions for consequences or reasons. Keyboard focus shows the highlight and an inset outline. The danger group is always last. The context menu mirrors the visible ⋯ menu exactly.

Search or jump

§8 · F-UX-029, F-UX-017

⌘K or Ctrl+K from anywhere. Destinations and actions filter instantly; records search after a 300 ms pause. Nothing in the palette dials, bills or publishes by itself: "Call Lead 1042…" opens the Call gate.

Toast

§9 · F-A11Y-014, F-FLOW-025, F-UX-014

For outcomes whose effect is off-screen. Inverse plane, one action, Dismiss. Success leaves after 6 s and pauses on hover or focus; Undo, error and progress toasts stay. Errors are assertive; everything else is polite. F8 jumps to them.

Notice and ConnectionBar

§10 · F-UX-019, F-QA-007, anti-pattern 18

Persistent conditions shown where they block, in five tones that all clear 5.4:1. role=status, never role=alert for a condition that was already true. The ConnectionBar is the only app-wide bar and it disappears when the network returns.

Wallet signal

§10.2 · F-UX-002, F-UX-028, F-RWD-013, F-QA-036, F-A11Y-015

The 42 px blue banner on every page becomes a four-rung ladder. The balance always lives in the Baseline (or the top-bar chip); a one-line, dismissible notice appears only on pages that place calls; and every Call action keeps its own reason, so dismissing never hides a block. Top up always opens the Top-up sheet, never Profile.

Inline status

§11 · F-UX-019, F-UX-023, F-UX-036, F-A11Y-019

The status grammar in one component: icon, sentence, quiet meta, one action. Failures sit under what failed, in plain words, with raw SDK or model text kept behind Details.

Spinner and skeletons

§12, §13 · F-UX-030, F-QA-007, F-VIS-023, F-FLOW-037

The shell never loads. Data regions show static skeletons after 200 ms that mirror their layout, never zeros and never invented chart data. Spinners are only for short, real requests, and are static under reduced motion.

Progress

§14 · F-UX-033, F-QA-022, F-FLOW-031

Determinate whenever there is a measure; a brief indeterminate bar only when there is none; named stages for anything long, such as an AI draft or indexing a file.

Empty states

§15 · F-VIS-023, F-UX-046, F-UX-042

A sentence and an action. First use, no results, filtered empty, all done and compact each have their own copy. No illustrations, mascots or emoji: the largest graphic is a 20 px icon.

Error states

§16 · F-UX-034, F-QA-017, F-QA-039, F-UX-036

Page, section, inline, offline, permission, degraded and session levels. Always inside the shell, always with Retry or a way out, never a silent redirect, and never an empty state standing in for a failed request.

Save state and Draft vs Live

§17, §18 · F-FLOW-003, F-QA-002, F-UX-012, F-QA-020

The save chip is a view of a state machine that can fail, so "Up to date while failing" becomes impossible. Draft is neutral, Live is green with a still dot, and forms show an Unsaved changes bar only when something changed.

Phone, at the 320 px floor

§1.7 · F-RWD-013, F-RWD-004, F-A11Y-005

Confirmations become bottom sheets with 44 px buttons; long menus become action sheets with the danger group last; toasts sit above the bottom bar; the wallet notice is one line with a separate 44 px Dismiss.
