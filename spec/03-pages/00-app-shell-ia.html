Laptop 1024 × 768: the rail

F-UX-007 · F-RWD-005 · F-VIS-015 · F-A11Y-017

At 1024–1279 the navigation is a 56 px rail with group separators instead of labels. Tooltips render in a portal on hover and keyboard focus, so they are never clipped and never widen the rail. Badge dots carry their words in the tooltip and the accessible name. The Baseline stays and switches to short forms.
