Direction proposal: journey-first, for teams new to voice AI

Clear Path

Every Vaani screen answers three questions in calm, plain language: where am I, what is true right now, and what is the one next step. Premium comes from effortlessness, not effects.

Where am IA labelled, grouped sidebar and one page header on every route.
What is trueStatus lines that are computed, never decorative. Draft is never shown as live.
What nextOne filled primary per region, and a readiness path before anything that costs money.
1

One next step

At most one filled button per region, and it is the next step of the journey: Import leads, Place test call, Publish.

2

Say what is true

Status comes from data. No "validated" on invalid flows, no "You're live" at ₹0, no hard-coded "online".

3

Plain words first

Sentence case, verbs on buttons, no HUD labels. Technical detail sits behind "Details", never in the way.

4

Reveal as you go

Defaults up front, advanced settings folded. Newcomers get guidance; experts get shortcuts and compact density.

5

Safe by default

Anything that dials customers or spends money goes preview, confirm, undo. Drafts never reach callers.

6

Calm hierarchy

One family, three weights, one accent. Depth from surfaces and hairlines. Colour only where it means state.

7

Same product everywhere

Every breakpoint is designed, every drag has a keyboard path, every state has an empty, loading and error design.

Signatures

Readiness path, status line and the When, Check, Do, End flow grammar. Nothing else is decorative.

Typography

Hanken Grotesk (kept from the marketing site) at 400, 500 and 600 only. JetBrains Mono is reserved for tokens a person reads character by character: phone numbers, timers, IDs, variables and keys. Noto Sans Devanagari carries Hindi at a taller line height. Nothing below 12 px.

Mono is allowed for

  • Phone numbers, always masked the same way
  • Timers and durations (02:14)
  • IDs, API keys, code, {{variables}}
  • Keycaps and language codes (HI, EN)

Uppercase is allowed for

  • Two-letter language codes
  • Key names inside keycaps
  • Nothing else: no tracked eyebrows, no caps buttons, no caps table headers

Always

  • Sentence case, no em-dash in chrome
  • Tabular numerals on every number
  • Negative tracking only at 18 px and above
  • 16 px inputs on touch screens

Colour

A cool mineral neutral ramp and one accent: Peacock, the teal already present in Vaani's logo and its old --peacock token. It is used for the primary action, focus, selection, links and the active nav item, and for nothing else. Semantic colours appear only for real state. Both themes are shown with fixed values; ratios are WCAG 2.x against the surface named.

Rules: text-3 never sits on surface-3 (4.49:1). Decorative lines (border, border-2) never carry meaning alone; anything a person must find to operate (input, checkbox, flow edge, port) uses border-strong at 3:1 or better. Dark mode has no shadows: depth is one surface step up plus a border.

Components and states

One button system (replacing today's three and 80 styles), one input, one badge set. Every state is designed, including focus (2 px Peacock ring, 2 px offset), disabled with a reason, and loading that keeps the label.
