Breakpoints

Each range is designed, not shrunk. Navigation always reaches all 12 sections.

Desktop · 1440+

232 px labelled sidebar, docked 320 px inspector, status line. Tables show all columns; Compact density available.

Laptop · 1024–1439

56 px rail with tooltips; [ expands it as an overlay. Below 1280 the inspector overlays the canvas; the minimap hides.

Tablet · 768–1023

Top bar with menu sheet (overlays, never pushes). One pane plus slide-over detail sheets. Flows open as outline plus read-only canvas and Test.

Mobile · 320–767

Top bar with status chip; bottom tabs Cockpit, Leads, Reports, Flows, More. Tables become two-line lists; inputs 16 px; 44 px targets.
