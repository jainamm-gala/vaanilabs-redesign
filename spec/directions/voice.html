A design direction for Vaani Labs

Bolchaalबोलचाल

Conversation is the interface. A calm ink-on-khadi workspace where colour and motion belong to the voice: peacock marks what Vaani says and does, each call draws its own shape as it happens, and every language appears in its own script.

  1. 01
    Only the voice movesLive colour and motion appear only while audio is flowing: a live call, a recording, a test run. Idle screens are still.
  2. 02
    Peacock means VaaniOne accent for the agent's voice, primary actions, focus and selection. Never a gradient, never decoration.
  3. 03
    Speak India nativelyScripts, lakh grouping, rupees and Hinglish are designed in, not translated in.
  4. 04
    Say only what is trueDraft or live, saved or failing, valid or not: every status comes from real state or is not shown.
  5. 05
    Every call has a costAnything that dials a real person shows who, which flow and roughly what it costs, then asks.
  6. 06
    Calm chrome, dense dataSentence case, one UI sans, mono only for tokens. Tables and transcripts get the density.
  7. 07
    Every hand reaches everythingKeyboard, screen reader, touch and 200% zoom reach every task, including building a flow and reading a call.

Typography

Three families, strict roles

Anek, from Mumbai's Ek Type, is one superfamily drawn across Latin and ten Indian scripts, so a Hindi turn and an English turn share one voice. It is used wide and confident for titles and numerals only. Hanken Grotesk, already the marketing face, carries every control and table. JetBrains Mono is kept for tokens: numbers you dial, IDs, timers and variables.

Rules: weights 400, 500, 600 only. Negative tracking only at 20 px and up, never positive. Uppercase only for acronyms as written (UPI, CRM, DND) and keycaps. Mono never for labels, headings or buttons. Numbers that change or are compared use tabular figures.

Colour

Khadi neutrals, one peacock

Neutrals are a barely warm "khadi" ramp (OKLCH hue 75, chroma under 0.01): paper and ink, never beige. Peacock is the single accent and keeps its hue in both themes, fixing today's blue-to-violet swap. It stands for Vaani: the agent's voice lane, primary actions, focus and selection. Green, amber and red are reserved for real state. Ratios below are WCAG 2 contrast, measured.

Signature elements

Three, each carrying data

The identity lives in three small devices that only appear where they mean something. None is decorative: each encodes call state, conversation structure or language, and each replaces something the audit found noisy (the idle orb, decorative status dots, letter-glyph source badges, mono language codes).

S1

The conversation line

Two thin lanes, agent above and caller below, drawn from diarised turns. It shows talk ratio, interruptions and silences at a glance, and grows only during a real call.

Mini, 96 x 12, in tables
  • Cockpit live card, with a now marker
  • Call Reports rows and the recording scrubber
  • Leads "Last call" column, Flow test panel
S2

The voice meter

Three bars, the brand's mark. Static at rest; it moves only when driven by real audio levels. It is the only live indicator in the product and replaces every decorative dot.

Live 01:272 live
  • Call state pill (Cockpit, Rep Console)
  • Sidebar count of live calls, meeting rooms
  • A node's "Running" chip during a test call
  • Static inside the logo mark
S3

Script chips

Languages are named in their own script: a glyph key plus the name. They are recognisable at a glance by any user, and they make code-switching (Hinglish) visible as a two-glyph chip.

Components

One of each, every state

One button system replaces today's three (80 styles). Controls are 32, 36 or 40 px tall (44 px on touch), 6 px radius, labels 14/500 in sentence case. Focus is always a 2 px peacock ring with 2 px offset. Disabled controls say why nearby. Only one filled primary per region.

Four breakpoints, four layouts

Designed, not shrunk

Each range changes structure, not just scale. Breakpoint tokens: 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440 px. Full-height shells use 100dvh; bottom bars respect safe-area insets; inputs are 16 px on touch.

Mobile · 320 to 767

Five-item bottom bar (Cockpit, Leads, Reports, Assistant, More). Tables become cards. Flow Designer is a read-only outline with Test call. Cockpit controls stick to the bottom. Wallet is a header chip.

Tablet · 768 to 1023

56 px icon rail; the labelled sidebar overlays instead of pushing. Tables keep a sticky first column. Flow Designer: pan, zoom, outline and Test call; editing starts at 1024 px. Cockpit stacks transcript over context.

Laptop · 1024 to 1439

Rail by default below 1280, labels from 1280. Flow palette collapses while the inspector is open; the inspector overlays the canvas below 1280. Minimap hides under a 600 px canvas.

Desktop · 1440 and up

232 px labelled sidebar, palette, canvas and inspector together. Reading pages cap at 1280 px; tables, canvas and Cockpit go full width.
