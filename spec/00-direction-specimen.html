Vaani Labs / Final design direction
Specimen · 27 Sep 2026

Sutradhar सूत्रधार

A calm, exact console for running voice agents. It is never wrong about what is live, what a call will cost, or what was said and in which language. The operator holds every thread; the agent speaks on stage.

Hanken Grotesk · JetBrains Mono · Noto Sans Devanagari Neel #1F4A94 Rows 40 / 32 / 48 px Radius 4 · 6 · 8 · 12 12 px floor
  1. 1Say only what is proven

    Every status is computed and written in words. No data, no element: "Live v7", "Couldn't save · Retry", never "SYS: ONLINE".

  2. 2Colour is state; shape is type

    Neel means operator intent. Green, amber and red mean call state. Flow steps differ by silhouette and glyph.

  3. 3Nothing dials, bills or goes live without a gate

    Call gate before any call, Publish gate before any flow goes live. Blocking checks give reasons; cost is a range.

  4. 4Dense where you scan, calm where you decide

    Standard 40 px, Compact 32 px, Touch 48 px rows on data. Forms stay in a calm 720 px column.

  5. 5Every action has an address

    Keyboard, pointer, touch and screen reader. Focus has an offset ring; selection has a fill. Every drag has a select.

  6. 6One frame, every breakpoint designed

    Sidebar, rail, nav sheet, bottom bar with More. All 12 sections reachable at every width.

  7. 7Quiet chrome; the work and the voice are loudest

    No gradients, glass, glows or idle loops. Only the live dot and real audio ever move.
